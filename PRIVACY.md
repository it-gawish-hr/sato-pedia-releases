# Sato Pharma · Pedia Line — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 27 October 2026*

Pedia Line is Sato Pharma's detail aid for Guamusin, Ivyronc and Lacto Advanced, used by Sato Pharma's medical representatives with healthcare professionals. This policy covers the Android app and its web version at https://sato-pedia.fly.dev/.

## Signing in

You sign in with your Sato Pharma work email. There is no password.

## What the app records

The app keeps a log of which screens are opened and for how long. Each entry is saved with:

- your work email;
- an identifier made for this installation of the app;
- the phone's make and model;
- the app's version.

The app does not use the phone's location, contacts or camera, and it shows no ads.

## Where it goes, and why

The log waits on the phone until there is a connection, then goes to Sato Pharma's own server at sato-pedia.fly.dev, hosted by Fly.io in Frankfurt, Germany. It travels encrypted (HTTPS). Sato Pharma uses it only to organise its field work. It is never sold and is not shared with anyone outside Sato Pharma.

## What stays on the phone

Your sign-in, the doctor's speciality chosen last, the text size, and any part of the log not yet sent. They are removed when the app is uninstalled.

## Deleting your data

To have your records deleted, email [info@satopharma.com](mailto:info@satopharma.com).

## When something else leaves the app

Only when you tap it:

- a link to the Egyptian Drug Authority's record or website opens in the phone's browser;
- the pharmacovigilance email address or hotline opens the phone's email or phone app;
- sharing a leaflet page sends that picture through the app you choose.

Those apps' own privacy policies then apply.

## Updates

The edition on Google Play is updated by Google Play. The edition downloaded from Sato Pharma's download page checks that page for a new version at most every six hours; the check sends no information about you or the phone.

## Children

The app is for healthcare professionals and is not directed at children.
